Account Deletion & Data Retention Policy

Official Data Policy for SwiftPOS

This page describes how users can request the deletion of their account and associated data for SwiftPOS, as well as our policy regarding data retention and permanent deletion.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion using either of the following two methods:

Method A: In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

Open the SwiftPOS app, navigate to Profile > tap Delete Account, and confirm your request.

Method B: Web / Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to support.swiftpos@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please send the email from the email address registered with your SwiftPOS account. Your request will be processed within 30 days.

2. Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data associated with your account will be permanently purged from our primary servers:

  • User account credentials (Name, Email Address, Password)
  • In-app profile preferences and user settings
  • Device tokens and active session logs

3. Data That May Be Retained & Retention Periods

Certain limited transactional or business operational records may be retained to fulfill legal, tax, fraud prevention, or financial regulatory requirements:

  • Invoicing and Purchase Records: Financial transaction histories (such as subscription status or billing records) will be retained in accordance with applicable tax and commercial laws for a period of up to 5 to 10 years.
  • Security and Audit Logs: Security logs may be preserved for a maximum of 90 days prior to automatic overwrite.
